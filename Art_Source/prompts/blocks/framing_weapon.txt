 손이 딸려 오면 그 손이 캐릭터의 손 위에 겹쳐 손이 두 개로 보인다.]

FRAMING:
a single object drawn alone, no character, no person, no figure,
no hands, no fingers, no arms, no gloves,
nothing holding it, nothing touching it, nothing behind it,
the object entirely inside the frame with an even margin on all four sides,
lit by the same single light source from the upper left as the reference image,
flat solid magenta #FF00FF background, perfectly uniform, no gradient no vignette,
no ground, no surface, no cast shadow, nothing under the object,
one object only, PNG

ONE OBJECT ONLY — do not draw a turnaround, do not draw multiple views,
do not draw a set or a rack or variants side by side,
do not draw smaller copies or inset detail views.
