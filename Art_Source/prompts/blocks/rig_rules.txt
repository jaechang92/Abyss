EMPTY HANDS — get this right first, before the pose:
draw both hands in full, open and unoccupied.
the near hand hangs loose at the end of the near arm, fingers relaxed and softly curled,
the empty magenta background readable through the curl of the fingers,
the far hand drawn the same way, rendered slightly darker.
each arm ends at its hand, and nothing continues past the hand.
he carries nothing and holds nothing — his gear is drawn in separate images
and is added back afterwards.

RIG BASE POSE — a neutral rest stance built to be deformed by a skeleton afterwards:
standing upright and relaxed, weight even on both feet,
head level and upright, the neck visible as its own segment between head and shoulders,
knees very slightly bent, never locked straight,
elbows slightly bent, never locked straight,
no limb crossing in front of another limb, no hand resting on the body, no arm folded

[🔴 아래 ARMS 절은 2026-08-20에 추가됐다. 예전 문구는 기하학적으로 불가능한 요구였다 —
 "정측면"과 "근접 팔과 몸통 사이에 배경이 비쳐야 한다"를 동시에 요구했는데,
 순수 측면에서 팔은 몸통 바로 위에 투영된다. 틈이 생기려면 **어깨에서 앞뒤로 벌려야** 한다.
 요구가 모순이면 모델은 둘 중 하나를 버리는데, 대개 버려지는 쪽이 정측면이었다(3/4로 샌다).
 다리도 같다 — 나란히 선 채로는 틈이 안 생기고, 살짝 앞뒤로 놓아야 열린다.]

ARMS — a pure side view puts the arms on top of the torso, so open them up on purpose:
the near arm swung slightly forward at the shoulder so it hangs clear in front of the torso,
the far arm swung slightly back at the shoulder so it hangs clear behind the torso,
a visible gap of empty magenta between each arm and the torso along its whole length,
both arms hanging down, not raised, not reaching, not folded,
the far arm fully visible along its whole length and rendered slightly darker
as the limb further from the light

LEGS — the same problem, the same answer:
the near leg placed slightly forward and the far leg slightly back,
a visible gap of empty magenta between the two legs along their whole length,
both feet flat and level on the same horizontal line, neither foot lifted,
the stride kept small so the stance still reads as standing at rest

JOINTS — these have to be findable:
the shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee and ankle each readable as a distinct point,
shoulders not buried under an oversized pauldron,
hips not hidden by a skirt or tabard hem, the waist line visible
