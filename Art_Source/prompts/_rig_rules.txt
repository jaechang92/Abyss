🔴 아래 지시는 취향이 아니라 SpriteSkin(2D Animation) 메시 변형의 제약이다. 빼지 말 것.
   · 그림 한 장이 메시 하나다. **겹쳐 그려진 팔·다리는 뼈로 못 떼어낸다** —
     팔이 몸통 위에 겹치면 팔 뼈를 돌릴 때 몸통 픽셀이 같이 딸려온다
   · 3/4 뷰는 팔다리가 단축(foreshortening)돼 있어 **회전시키면 길이가 어긋나 보인다**.
     정측면이어야 뼈 회전이 실제 팔다리 움직임으로 읽힌다
   · 무기가 다리를 가로지르면 무기와 다리가 **한 덩어리로 용접**된다
   · 펄럭이는 천은 base에 이미 움직임이 박힌 것이라, 뼈로 흔들면 두 번 흔들린다

OVERRIDE THE FRAMING ABOVE: this is a pure flat side profile, NOT a three-quarter view.
The camera is exactly level with the character and exactly perpendicular to the direction
he faces. Facing right. No part of the body turns toward the viewer.
No foreshortening anywhere — every limb is seen at its full length.

RIG BASE POSE — a neutral rest stance built to be deformed by a skeleton afterwards:
standing upright and relaxed, weight even on both feet, both feet flat at the same height,
the near leg and the far leg clearly separated, with a visible gap of empty magenta
between them along their whole length,
knees very slightly bent, never locked straight,
the near arm hanging free beside the body, with a visible gap of empty magenta
between that arm and the torso along its whole length,
the far arm fully visible and separated as well, rendered slightly darker
as the limb further from the light,
elbows slightly bent, never locked straight,
head level and upright, the neck visible as its own segment between head and shoulders,
no limb crossing in front of another limb, no limb crossing in front of the torso,
no hand resting on the body, no arm folded

WEAPON:
the weapon held out clear of the body, its blade or shaft must not cross the legs,
must not cross the far arm, and must not overlap the torso,
if the weapon is long, angle it away from the body into empty magenta space

CLOTH:
cloak, tabard, scarf and ragged hems hang straight down at rest, settled and still,
not flaring, not blowing, not billowing, not caught in wind,
cloth must not wrap around or hide the legs, the leg silhouettes stay readable

a single static neutral pose, one figure only, no action, no motion blur,
no effects, no particles, no glow trails, no impact
