OBJECT PURPOSE — this image becomes a rigid part attached to a character rig:

RIGID:
the object is drawn straight and undeformed, no bending, no warping,
no perspective distortion, no lens effect, no dramatic camera angle,
flat and square to the viewer

GRIP — the place a hand or an arm would hold it:
clearly visible and completely unobstructed,
drawn as a distinct wrap, band, cord or strap so that its centre can be located later,
nothing covering it, no hand, no glove, no fingers

INTEGRITY:
the whole object inside the frame, nothing cropped, nothing cut off by the frame edge,
no part of it running off the side of the image

SCALE AND STYLE:
same linework weight, same cel shading steps and same light direction
as the reference character,
the object's glowing accent is the same colour it has on the character

NO EFFECTS:
no motion trail, no swing arc, no impact spark, no particles, no aura, no smoke,
the only light it emits is the glow built into the object itself
